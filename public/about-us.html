About KDM Express

Building Nepal's Next-Generation Courier & Delivery Ecosystem

Our Mission & Vision

Connecting Individuals & Businesses Across Nepal

KDM Express is dedicated to delivering reliable, affordable, and technology-driven courier services tailored to Nepal. From fast intra-city document dispatches in Kathmandu Valley to nationwide e-commerce COD deliveries in Pokhara, Chitwan, Butwal, Biratnagar, and beyond, we bridge distances with accountability.

Our platform combines real-time consignment milestone scanning, dedicated branch counters, and merchant dashboard tools to empower Nepal's growing commerce sector.
