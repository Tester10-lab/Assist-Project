Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shipping with KDM Express in Nepal

How do I track my KDM Express consignment?

Enter your tracking number (e.g. KDM-2026-000123) on our Track Shipment page to view live milestone updates from pickup to final delivery.

What currency are shipping rates charged in?

All courier charges and Cash-on-Delivery (COD) collections are processed in Nepalese Rupees (NPR).

Does KDM Express provide Cash-on-Delivery (COD) for online sellers?

Yes! We provide complete COD delivery services for e-commerce merchants across Nepal with fast bank transfer settlements and portal reporting.

How long does same-day delivery take in Kathmandu Valley?

Same-Day Express shipments requested before 11:00 AM are delivered on the same afternoon within Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur.

How can I contact KDM Express support?

You can reach our customer support team directly via Call or WhatsApp at +977 980-1081469 or email info@kdmexpress.com.
